How it works

  1. Register

    Fill out the form with your college, major, interests, and skills.

  2. Get matched

    We place you on a project team based on the sector and skills you pick.

  3. Build with a mentor

    Work with a partner company, guided by an industry mentor.

  4. Show your work

    Finished projects can be featured, and you can join the talent pool.

What you get

  • Real project experience with partner companies
  • Mentorship from people working in the field
  • A portfolio piece you can talk about in interviews
  • Optional spot in our talent pool, shared only with approved recruiters
  • A community of students from many schools and backgrounds

What the form asks

  • Name, email, university, college, major, and class year
  • Sectors you're interested in (AI, data, cybersecurity, fintech, and more)
  • Skills and tools you already use
  • Whether to join the talent pool (you can leave anytime)
  • Optional background questions, reported only as group totals

How a partnership works

  1. Reach out

    Send a short project idea through the form or by email.

  2. Scope it together

    We agree on goals, timeline, and what you'll receive.

  3. Team gets to work

    A student team and mentor build it, with check-ins along the way.

  4. Delivery and feedback

    You receive the final work and share a short rating of the team.

What we ask from you

  • A project brief and one point of contact
  • A few short check-ins during the project
  • A quick feedback form when the project ends
  • Your OK before we showcase the project or quote you

How recruiting works

  1. Request access

    Tell us your company, roles, and timeline.
